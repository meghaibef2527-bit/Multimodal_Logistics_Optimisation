# ATS-Friendly CV Bullets

- Built a Python-based multimodal logistics optimisation model across **5,000 shipments, 516 candidate routes and 804 route legs**, minimising shipment-specific logistics cost subject to SLA and leg-level capacity constraints.
- Recalculated tonne-kilometre transport costs from route-leg data and independently validated route recommendations against the supplied reference output, identifying **18 route-level discrepancies** without forcing model agreement.
- Developed Excel and Power BI-ready analytics covering **SLA feasibility, multimodal mode mix, road-baseline savings and sensitivity scenarios** for fuel-cost shocks, tighter SLAs, capacity constraints and shipment-weight changes.
